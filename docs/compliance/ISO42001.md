# ISO/IEC 42001 alignment

**Position:** TrustCircle is designed to support an organisation in implementing and operating an
ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system (AIMS) and in preparing for its certification audit. It maps
its shipped controls and evidence to the clauses and Annex A control areas of the standard.

**Claim boundary:** using TrustCircle does not by itself make an organisation conformant with or
certified to ISO/IEC 42001. Certification is issued only by a certification body accredited for
ISO/IEC 42001 (assessing under ISO/IEC 17021-1 and ISO/IEC 42006). Many AIMS requirements are
organisational, such as leadership commitment, competence and management review. Software can
record and evidence them, but it cannot fulfil them. The product follows the same rule in its UI
(`apps/web/src/features/compliance/claim-labels.ts`): readiness is never labelled as certification.

Standard text is copyrighted. This page refers to clauses by identifier only and describes each
obligation in our own words. Check identifiers against the edition your assessor uses.

## How to read the mapping

- **Supports**: TrustCircle provides a control that operates on your AI estate and produces
  evidence an assessor can follow.
- **Records**: the obligation is met by your organisation. TrustCircle holds the record, its owner
  and its evidence.
- **Out of scope**: you need to meet it outside the product.

## Readiness evidence in this repository

- `tests/fixtures/audit-readiness/`: a sample, non-certified ISO/IEC 42001, 42005 and 42006
  readiness package, bound by a signed manifest and validated by `npm run test:audit-readiness`.
- `docs/governance/known-gaps.md`: what the platform does not do, with owners and review dates.
- `docs/security/SANITIZATION_REPORT.md`: how this repository is kept free of sensitive data.
- `.provision/receipt-*.json`, created per consumer instance: provisioning evidence for clause 8.1
  and Annex A.6.

## For adopting organisations

To get from readiness to certification you still need to:

1. Define your AIMS scope, AI policy and objectives, and record them in TrustCircle.
2. Assign owners and competence evidence for every role.
3. Run at least one full cycle of risk assessment, impact assessment, internal audit and management
   review, with records.
4. Engage an accredited certification body for a stage 1 and stage 2 audit.
